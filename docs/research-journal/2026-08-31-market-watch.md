## Key learning observations
1. A strong earnings surprise does not establish durable post-event price confirmation after one trading session.
2. Cross-asset institutional-flow divergence can provide a distinct observation: BTC flows weakened while ETH flows remained positive.
3. Geopolitical energy moves should be monitored as research candidates but separated from durable asset-signal confirmation because the catalyst can reverse quickly.
4. Contradictory price evidence must remain visible rather than being rationalized away by strong fundamental evidence.

## Candidate rule for experimentation
> Earnings beat + 2–3 session hold above the post-event breakout zone + supportive relative volume may be a better test for durable post-event price confirmation than earnings beat + one strong session.

This is a **research hypothesis**, not a production scoring rule. It must be evaluated on historical and prospective observations before any production weight is assigned.
